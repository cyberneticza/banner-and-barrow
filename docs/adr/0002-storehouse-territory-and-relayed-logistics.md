# Territory comes from Storehouses; stock is local and relayed

Territory is the reach of a Player's Storehouses (the Keep being the first and largest), not of towers or of every building, and each Storehouse holds its own stock. Shortfalls become Demands that are relayed Storehouse-to-Storehouse by the needing Storehouse's Carriers, with Reservations locking the stock en route. We chose this over a global stockpile because it makes expansion, supply lines and raids matter (destroy a Storehouse and its buildings burn unless the Ruin is repaired), which is the Settlers identity the game is built on.

## Considered Options

- Global stockpile with cosmetic carriers: far simpler, but removes logistics as gameplay.
- Settlers 2 road-segment carriers: rejected as a second graph/traffic system on top of flow fields.

## Consequences

- Recruitment and upgrades currently withdraw instantly from the nearest Storehouses (a deliberate v1 simplification); construction and production inputs are physically carried.
- Enemy Presence only blocks building/repair; it never flips Territory.
